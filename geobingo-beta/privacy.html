geobingo — privacy

geobingo is a geography game. You can play without an account; your progress then stays only on your device.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in with Google lets your XP count on the leaderboards. We ask Google only for an anonymous ID that is unique to you in this app (OpenID Connect scope openid). We do not receive or store your email address, your Google name or your photo.

We store: a scrambled (hashed) form of that anonymous ID, the display name you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboards), and the XP you earn while signed in. The data is kept in Google Cloud (Firestore, EU region).

To delete your data, email rgkiran@gmail.com with your display name.

Datenschutz (Deutsch)

Die Anmeldung ist freiwillig. Von Google erhalten wir nur eine anonyme ID (Scope openid) – keine E-Mail-Adresse, keinen Namen, kein Foto. Gespeichert werden eine verschlüsselte (gehashte) Form dieser ID, der von dir gewählte, öffentlich sichtbare Name und deine XP (Google Cloud, EU). Löschung auf Anfrage an rgkiran@gmail.com.
