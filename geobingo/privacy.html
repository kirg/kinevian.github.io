geobingo — privacy

geobingo is a geography game. You can play without an account; your progress then stays only on your device.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in with Google lets your XP count on the leaderboards. We ask Google for an ID that is unique to you in this app and for your email address (OpenID Connect scopes openid email). We do not receive or store your Google name or your photo.

We store: a scrambled (hashed) form of that ID, your email address (private: never shown to other players or shared — it lets us reach you about your account), the display name you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboards), and the XP you earn while signed in. The data is kept in Google Cloud (Firestore, EU region).

Instead of Google you can sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email address (no password). We keep that email address privately, the same way.

Profile

If you sign in, your profile — display name, XP, badges, crowns (fastest times) and game statistics such as your day streak — can be seen by everyone and shared as a link. Your email address is never shown on it. You can change your name and email address on the profile page.

Progress backup

While you're signed in, your game progress (XP, streaks, which countries you've practised, best times and scores, badges and your app settings) is backed up to your account, so it comes back when you sign in again — also on another device. Signing out clears it from the device; it stays in your account.

Usage statistics

To see how geobingo is used, the app sends anonymous counts: a random device ID created by the app (not linked to you unless you're signed in), the app version, language, and events such as "a Flags quiz finished, 8 of 10 right". No names, no location, no advertising or tracking by third parties.

Game log

Each game, flashcards run and Learn visit is also logged, to see which features are used and to re-score fairly if the rules for XP or badges change: what you played (topic, answer style, regions and other options), how it went (score, XP, time) and, for quizzes, each answer (which country, right or wrong, how you answered, how long it took). It's linked to your account if you're signed in, otherwise to the random device ID. No location, no voice recordings (only whether you answered by voice).

Game log entries are kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically (entries from the current season are kept until it ends, as they may be needed to re-score it).

To delete your data, email rgkiran@gmail.com with your display name.

This also deletes your game log.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app (Settings → About), we store what you wrote together with the app version, language and device type (e.g. "iPhone, Safari") — nothing that identifies you. Please don't put personal details in the message.

Datenschutz (Deutsch)

Die Anmeldung ist freiwillig. Von Google erhalten wir eine ID und deine E-Mail-Adresse (Scopes openid email) – keinen Namen, kein Foto. Gespeichert werden eine verschlüsselte (gehashte) Form dieser ID, deine E-Mail-Adresse (privat, nie für andere sichtbar), der von dir gewählte, öffentlich sichtbare Name und deine XP (Google Cloud, EU). Dein Profil (Name, XP, Abzeichen, Kronen, Statistik wie deine Tagesserie) ist für alle sichtbar und als Link teilbar – deine E-Mail nie. Solange du angemeldet bist, wird dein Spielfortschritt (XP, Serien, geübte Länder, Bestzeiten, Abzeichen, Einstellungen) in deinem Konto gesichert; beim Abmelden wird er vom Gerät gelöscht und kommt bei der nächsten Anmeldung zurück. Die App sendet außerdem anonyme Nutzungszahlen (zufällige Geräte-ID, Version, Sprache, z. B. „Flaggen-Quiz beendet, 8 von 10 richtig“) und protokolliert jedes Spiel, jeden Karteikarten-Durchgang und jeden Besuch in „Lernen“ (Thema, Optionen, Ergebnis, XP, Zeit, bei Quizzen jede Antwort – welches Land, richtig oder falsch, wie beantwortet, wie lange), verknüpft mit deinem Konto, wenn du angemeldet bist, sonst mit der zufälligen Geräte-ID – um zu sehen, was genutzt wird, und um bei neuen Regeln fair neu zu werten. Kein Standort, keine Sprachaufnahmen. Löschung auf Anfrage an rgkiran@gmail.com.

Feedback aus der App (Einstellungen → Über) wird mit App-Version, Sprache und Gerätetyp gespeichert – ohne Angaben, die dich identifizieren.
